How It Works

Three simple steps to create engaging video experiences

1

Create QR Codes

Upload your video URLs and generate beautiful, customizable QR codes. Bulk import from directories or create individual codes.

2

Scan & Watch

Visitors scan the QR code with their phone. Videos auto-launch in fullscreen with landscape orientation for the best experience.

3

Seamless Experience

In-player QR scanner lets viewers discover more content without leaving fullscreen. Perfect for multi-video experiences.
